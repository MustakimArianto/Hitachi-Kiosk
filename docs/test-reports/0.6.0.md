0.6.0 is implemented against the fakes. **Printing is not part of it**: on 2026-10-25 the PO removed printing and all settings work from 0.6.0 (D-36). Complete, the Upsell strip (with "Make it a meal") and the Budget Plan improvements are built.

## Scope and what was built

- **Complete** (DEV-060, FR-020): flood, badge, check, rings, confetti, digit cascade, the order-type instruction line, the 20 s countdown ring and Done; the session is cleared before Idle appears. It takes over the payment hand-off: Complete acknowledges the paid payment itself once it is shown, Checkout no longer calls `acknowledgePaid`, and an order recovered as paid reaches it through the new `RecoveredPaidSource` (implemented by `PendingOrderRecoveryCoordinator`). The pending order is now cleared only after Complete is shown, so the 0.5.0 known limit (a kill between Checkout and Complete leaves no receipt) is gone; a recovered order still has no lines and no order type (TD-24). The receipt-printer illustration is a decorative demo visual only; nothing prints and there is no print-failed card.
- **Upsell strip** (DEV-105, DEV-106, DEV-114, FR-014): `Upsell.suggestions`, `withinBudget`, `cards`, `strip` and `mealSwitch`, `Cart.switchOne`, `OrderSession.switchOne`; `shouldOffer` and `upsellShown` are removed; the SCR-09 route, screen, ViewModel and goldens are removed. The strip is on Your order below the lines and is hidden while a Pay blocker shows. A card the customer added keeps its slot. Plan orders show "Fits your budget" and are never meal-upgraded. The "Make it a meal" card uses the optional modifier group `purpose` = `MEAL_UPGRADE` (OQ-17 resolved; api-spec, `openapi.yaml` and fixtures updated). Room is at **version 3** with `MIGRATION_2_3` adding `modifier_group.purpose` (TD-25): an already cached menu gets the meal card only after a menu refresh.
- **Budget Plan** (DEV-107–DEV-112, FR-032): Step 1 (2 adults, "About Rp X per person", dock "Choose a budget to continue", keypad on the current amount with "Minimum Rp 1.000"); Step 2 (two plan cards, "What's included" open with one row per person and Swap, chips "Add sides" / "Add desserts", the switch "Allow a little over budget", "Reset to suggested", "Combo savings"); the Customize sheet is deleted and replaced by the swap sheet (`BudgetPlanSwapSheet`, `BudgetPlanner.alternatives`); `OrderSession.replacePlan`, `acknowledgePlanAdded`, `PlanTag.quantities` and `OrderState.planAdded`; the cart-limit message with "Review order"; the hand-off "Added to order" for 0.6 s then Your order with the green "Plan added" pill for 2.6 s; "Edit plan" through `BudgetPlanRoute(edit)`; the "Plan by budget" pill in the Menu category bar on every tab, hidden when `budgetPlanner.enabled` is false (TD-26). OQ-15: the combo side rule is applied, the "cap at min(people, 4)" half is not (the golden cases need one extra per person), and the planner and pricing suites are unchanged. OQ-16: no preselected chip.
- **Debug only**: the demo images are now 28 (23 product photos, the meal photo, 3 idle slides and the logo).
- **Not built (D-36)**: DEV-055, DEV-057, DEV-058, DEV-059, DEV-061 and TC-091–TC-095, TC-099, TC-100. DEV-056 and FR-021/FR-022 now wait for OQ-19 (build printing later or drop it from 1.0).

## Deviations from the design, recorded

- **Strip**: the design freezes the strip; the built app re-ranks it as the order changes (DEV-106 wording), except that a card the customer added keeps its slot.
- **Complete**: the receipt-printer illustration is a demo visual only. There is no print-failed card, so `S_Complete_printFailed` is not built and FR-020 AC4 does not apply. The instruction line for an order recovered after a restart is neutral because the order type is not stored.
- **Budget Plan**: the dock button "Review order" and the hero copy follow the design ("Within your budget" / "Best fit for your table"; "A little extra" / "Slightly above your budget"; "More than enough" with the title "Our menu tops out at Rp X…" and the badge "Rp X under your budget"); "Added to order" shows for 0.6 s; "Minimum Rp 1.000" sits under the keypad amount and is the disabled button label. Where the PRD said otherwise, the PRD was corrected.
- **Edit mode** keeps the dock copy "Add plan to order" / "Added to order" (invented, A-50).
