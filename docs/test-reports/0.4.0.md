## Code review

An independent review of the 0.4.0 diff found five issues, all fixed before this report:
- a swap could leave the over cap;
- a plan could be added twice during the 1 s "Added" state;
- planning ran on the main thread and slowed down with prices that share only Rp 1;
- Back from Upsell left the order in Reviewing;
- "View order" could push its screen twice.

## Deviations from the design, recorded

- **SCR-09:** the strip and "Continue" show the cart subtotal, as drawn. The cards follow the "exclude cart items" rule, so the sample cards differ from the artboard's.
- **SCR-10:**
  - The offline banner is the white banner with a spinner from `Payment.dc.html`, not the Menu's amber banner.
  - A line's options show only what differs from the defaults.
  - "Price updated · was Rp X" shows the old line total.
  - The Promo row uses the config label.
- **SCR-19:**
  - The step 2 title follows the PRD.
  - The group card uses the PRD copy.
  - The kids' menu note is a note line on the hero card (there is no board for it).
  - The screen opens with no budget, 1 adult and 0 children (A-43).
  - The hero ring is drawn inside the card, and the sheet footer glow is left out.
- **SCR-13:**
  - On API 30 (no blur) the scrim is darker.
  - At 0 the app goes to Idle at once, without the design's 400 ms "0".
  - The `countdownLow` golden is captured with reduce motion, so the pulse is not visible in it.
