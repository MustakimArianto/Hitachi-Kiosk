## Instrumented suite (`connectedDebugAndroidTest`)

**TBD by the lead:** final pass and fail counts and the emulator. The suite has 38 tests: the 31 of 0.4.0 plus the new `CheckoutUiTest` (7). Backend, serial number, connectivity and the card terminal are scriptable fakes (`FakeBindingsTestModule`); the data layer, Room, DataStore and the Keystore-backed token store are real.

History, kept for honesty: the first run on the API 37 emulator passed 12 of the 38 tests and failed 26. The cause was one defect: the pending-order recovery gate (Idle ignores "tap to order" while a recovery asks the backend) only lifted when `KioskApp.start()` ran, and the instrumented tests use `HiltTestApplication`, which never calls it. The coordinator now starts its own consumers when the startup run is requested, and a unit test covers it (`without start() the startup run still settles and lifts the gate`). The fix is in; the result of the run after it is the **TBD by the lead** figure above.

## Review fixes

A review of the 0.5.0 diff led to three fix commits, each with its tests, and `verify` stayed green:
- the payment machine: the terminal is cancelled whenever the backend ends the payment, "Confirming payment" is bounded by 60 s, an E9 amount that is not the order total is cancelled, and a stale state of an earlier order is reset (TC-185, TC-187–TC-189);
- the order repository: a new order never overwrites a pending order whose payment is unsettled, and networks the platform has not validated end OFFLINE (TC-186, TC-190);
- recovery: it starts from the coordinator, not from Splash, and every deferral is retried (TC-191, TC-192).

## Deviations from the design, recorded

- **Checkout (SCR-11):**
  - The QR centre mark is 96 dpx (20 % of the code); the design draws 104 dpx (21.7 %).
  - The real level-H QR is denser than the design's pseudo QR.
  - The card shadow is `ink` at 0.06 alpha through the floating elevation.
  - The switcher thumb shadow bleeds slightly, and the entrance and shake animations are approximated.
  - The QR and card goldens have no simulate bar (it is debug-only, and the goldens follow the release build).
  - A card payment cancelled on the terminal shows "Payment cancelled" / "The payment was cancelled on the terminal. Try again or choose another method." The design text is QR-only. **Needs PO confirmation (A-45).**
  - The "Your order is saved" note shows only on the "Ordering is paused" variant.
- **Your order (SCR-10):** unchanged from 0.4.0, now fed from `OrderState.issues`; Pay is disabled with its reason while an issue blocks or the kiosk is OFFLINE.
